YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

How Midbol Care collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information across its services.

SECTION 01

Introduction

BYBL Integrated Services Limited, trading as Midbol Care (“Midbol Care”, “BYBL”, “we”, “us” or “our”), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, shared and protected when you use the Midbol Care mobile applications, website and related services.

Midbol Care is a digital care-management platform designed to help health and social care organisations manage their daily operations, including care records, staff scheduling, attendance, care visits, medication records and related administrative activities.

Our services may be accessed through iOS, Android and web applications.

Because Midbol Care may process sensitive health and care information, we recognise the importance of protecting the privacy, confidentiality and rights of individuals whose information is managed through the platform.

This policy should be read alongside any privacy notices provided by the care organisation responsible for your care or employment.

SECTION 03

Our Data Protection Responsibilities

Midbol Care may act as either a data processor or a data controller, depending on the information involved and why it is processed.

When acting as a data processor

When a care organisation uses Midbol Care to manage information about its service users, employees or care operations, that organisation will ordinarily be the data controller.

Midbol Care processes that information on the organisation's behalf, in accordance with applicable contractual arrangements, documented instructions and data protection requirements.

The care organisation is generally responsible for determining why the information is collected, the appropriate lawful basis for processing it and how long it should be retained.

When acting as a data controller

Midbol Care may independently determine how certain information is processed for purposes such as account administration, customer enquiries, business communications, subscription management and the security of its own services.

In those circumstances, BYBL is responsible for complying with the applicable data protection obligations.

SECTION 04

Information We Collect and Process

The information processed through Midbol Care depends on the features used and the information provided by the relevant care organisation.

4.1 Personal and contact information

This may include:

  • Full name, date of birth and contact details.
  • Residential address and emergency contact information.
  • Username, email address and account identifiers.
  • Organisation, employment role and assigned permissions.

4.2 Care and health information

Care organisations may use the platform to process:

  • Care plans and individual support requirements.
  • Daily care notes and visit records.
  • Health conditions and relevant medical information.
  • Medication and medication administration records.
  • Risk assessments, body maps and incident reports.
  • Safeguarding and other care-related documentation.
  • Uploaded documents, photographs and attachments where the relevant functionality is available and used.

Health and care information may constitute special-category personal data under UK GDPR and requires additional safeguards.

4.3 Staff and operational information

This may include staff schedules, visit assignments, attendance records, clock-in and clock-out times, activity records and relevant employment information.

4.4 Technical and account information

We may process information required for authentication, account security and service operation, including account identifiers, login activity, device or browser information, IP addresses, error information and relevant system activity records.

The precise technical information collected depends on the application and services in use.

SECTION 05

How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when:

  • A care organisation creates or manages an account.
  • An authorised user enters information into the platform.
  • Care records, documents or attachments are uploaded.
  • A staff member uses attendance or location-dependent functions.
  • A user signs in or interacts with the application.
  • Someone contacts Midbol Care for support or business enquiries.
  • The platform generates information necessary for its operation and security.

Care organisations are responsible for ensuring that information they enter into Midbol Care is collected and used lawfully.

SECTION 06

How We Use Personal Information

Personal information may be processed to:

  • Provide and maintain the Midbol Care platform.
  • Support care planning, documentation and record management.
  • Manage staff schedules, visits and attendance.
  • Administer user accounts and permissions.
  • Enable authorised communication and operational activities.
  • Support relevant location-based attendance verification.
  • Respond to customer enquiries and technical support requests.
  • Protect accounts, investigate security incidents and maintain appropriate records.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal health or care information to advertisers.

Personal information collected for care-management purposes is not used by Midbol Care for third-party behavioural advertising.

SECTION 07

Camera, Photographs and Uploaded Documents

Midbol Care is designed to support care-related documentation, which may include photographs, body maps, incident evidence and other relevant attachments.

Where camera, photo-library or file-upload functionality is available in the application, authorised users may use it to submit relevant information in accordance with their organisation's care procedures and applicable legal requirements.

Photographs and attachments may contain sensitive personal information, including health information.

Such information must be collected and uploaded only where appropriate and lawful, with any necessary consent or other legal authorisation.

Where the application requests camera or photo-library access, the permission is intended to support the relevant user-initiated functionality.

Users can manage applicable permissions through their device settings.

Midbol Care does not require users to provide unrestricted access to their personal photo libraries merely to use unrelated application features.

The availability of specific camera and photo functions may vary according to the application version and device.

SECTION 08

Location Information and GPS

Midbol Care may use device location information to support staff attendance verification.

When a staff member uses a location-dependent clock-in or clock-out feature, the application may request access to the device's location to establish whether the attendance action is taking place at the relevant care location.

Depending on the attendance configuration, location-validation information may form part of the attendance record.

Location information is used for legitimate workforce and attendance-management purposes on behalf of the relevant care organisation.

Location access is subject to the permissions granted by the user and the functionality of the device.

Users may disable location access through their device settings. Doing so may prevent location-dependent attendance features from operating correctly.

This policy does not authorise continuous employee tracking or unrelated location monitoring.

SECTION 09

Mobile Application Permissions

The Midbol Care applications may request device permissions where necessary for supported features.

These may include:

Location: To support location-dependent staff attendance verification.

Camera: To capture photographs or documents where supported by the application.

Photos and files: To select or upload care-related attachments where supported.

Notifications: To provide relevant account, operational or care-management notifications where enabled.

Biometric authentication: Where supported, device-based authentication may be used to assist secure sign-in. Biometric verification is ordinarily handled by the device operating system rather than requiring Midbol Care to receive raw fingerprint or facial biometric data.

Permissions are requested according to the features supported by the relevant application version.

Users can review and manage applicable permissions through their iOS or Android device settings.

SECTION 10

Legal Basis for Processing

Where Midbol Care acts as a data controller, processing is carried out under an appropriate lawful basis under UK GDPR.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests or consent where appropriate.

Where Midbol Care processes information on behalf of a care organisation, the care organisation is ordinarily responsible for establishing the applicable lawful basis and any additional condition required for processing special-category information.

Health and care information must be handled in accordance with applicable UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 requirements.

SECTION 11

Sharing Personal Information

Personal information may be shared with or accessed by:

Authorised care organisation personnel: Staff members, managers and administrators who require access for their authorised responsibilities.

Midbol Care personnel: Authorised personnel who require access for legitimate administration, technical support, security or operational purposes, subject to appropriate restrictions.

Technology providers: Infrastructure, hosting, communications and other technical service providers involved in delivering the platform.

Microsoft Azure is used for application hosting and associated infrastructure.

Microsoft services are also involved in certain system email communications. During testing, email arrangements associated with Lotus Beta Analytics have been used. The use of such services does not, by itself, mean that Lotus Beta Analytics is authorised to access care records.

Professional advisers and authorities: Information may be disclosed where legally required or appropriately necessary for professional, regulatory or legal purposes.

We do not disclose customer-controlled care records to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

SECTION 12

Data Storage and International Processing

Midbol Care uses Microsoft Azure infrastructure to support its services.

The application infrastructure has included resources hosted in Microsoft's Central US region, while an identified Azure storage account is hosted in UK South.

The company is reviewing and implementing arrangements for UK-based production hosting.

Different technical services may process information in different locations, depending on the infrastructure, integrations and services involved.

Where personal information is transferred or made accessible outside the United Kingdom, BYBL and the relevant data controllers must assess the applicable legal requirements and implement appropriate safeguards where required.

These may include recognised international data transfer mechanisms and contractual protections.

We do not represent that all personal information is stored exclusively within the United Kingdom.

SECTION 13

How We Protect Personal Information

We recognise the importance of protecting information processed through Midbol Care, particularly confidential health and care records.

Our approach to security includes appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure.

Depending on the relevant system and configuration, these measures may include:

  • Secure communications between applications and services.
  • Authentication and access restrictions.
  • Role-based permissions.
  • Encryption and security controls provided by the underlying infrastructure.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Security-related records and operational procedures.
  • Incident investigation and response arrangements.

Security controls are reviewed as the platform develops and its infrastructure changes.

No internet-connected application or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

SECTION 14

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.

Where a care organisation controls the information, that organisation ordinarily determines the appropriate retention period for its care and employment records.

Midbol Care supports the relevant organisation's lawful retention and deletion instructions, subject to applicable obligations.

Certain care records may need to be retained for legally required periods, including after a staff member or service user stops using the platform.

Account information, operational records, security information and backup copies may be subject to different retention periods.

Information that is no longer required should be deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled in accordance with the applicable retention arrangements.

SECTION 15

Account Deletion and Personal Data Requests

Users may request closure or deletion of their Midbol Care accounts by contacting:

support@midbolcare.co.uk

Where account deletion functionality is available within the application, users may also initiate a request through the relevant account-management feature.

Requests concerning accounts administered by a care organisation may need to be coordinated with that organisation.

Account deletion does not necessarily require immediate deletion of every care record, attendance record or other document associated with the account.

Some information may need to be retained by the relevant care organisation for legal, safeguarding, regulatory or legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

Where deletion is authorised and legally appropriate, Midbol Care will take the relevant steps within its responsibilities to process the request.

Information retained for legitimate legal purposes will remain subject to appropriate protection and access restrictions.

SECTION 16

Access, Correction and Export of Information

Individuals may request access to or correction of their personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Where information is controlled by a care organisation, requests should ordinarily be directed to that organisation.

Care organisations may also request relevant data exports or assistance retrieving their information, subject to their contractual arrangements and the technical capabilities available.

Requests for information export or account closure may be submitted through the relevant organisation or to Midbol Care using the privacy contact details provided in this policy.

SECTION 17

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure where legally applicable.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis.
  • Exercise rights relating to certain automated decisions.

These rights may be subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

If your request concerns information controlled by a care organisation, we may refer you to that organisation or assist it in responding.

For information controlled directly by BYBL, contact:

support@midbolcare.co.uk

Applicable requests will be handled within statutory time limits, normally one calendar month, subject to lawful extensions.

SECTION 18

Children and Vulnerable Individuals

Midbol Care is designed for professional use by authorised care organisations and their personnel.

It is not intended as a general consumer application for children.

However, care organisations may use the platform to manage information concerning children or vulnerable individuals where lawful and appropriate.

Such information requires particular care and must be processed in accordance with applicable data protection and safeguarding obligations.

SECTION 19

Analytics, Advertising and Automated Decisions

Midbol Care does not use personal health or care information for third-party behavioural advertising.

We do not sell health information or care records.

Operational or technical information may be processed where necessary to maintain, support and protect the service.

Any use of third-party analytics, diagnostic tools or other technologies must be reflected in the applicable privacy disclosures.

Midbol Care does not represent itself as an automated medical diagnosis or clinical decision-making service.

SECTION 20

Data Breaches and Security Incidents

Where Midbol Care becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting information processed on behalf of a customer organisation, it will notify the relevant organisation without undue delay in accordance with applicable obligations.

Where BYBL acts as a data controller, it will assess its reporting obligations under UK GDPR.

Where a breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, notification to the Information Commissioner's Office may be required, generally within 72 hours of awareness where feasible.

Affected individuals may also need to be notified where the relevant legal threshold is met.

Third-Party Websites and Services

The Midbol Care website or applications may contain links to external services.

Third-party websites and services operate under their own privacy policies.

We encourage users to review those policies before providing personal information to external providers.
