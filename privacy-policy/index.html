SECTION 01

Introduction

BYBL Integrated Services Limited, trading as Midbol Care ('Midbol Care', 'we', 'us' or 'our'), is committed to protecting the personal information of everyone who interacts with or whose information is processed through our platform.

Midbol Care is a cloud-based care management platform designed for organisations providing health and social care services. It enables authorised users to manage care records, medication records, staff information, rotas, visits, attendance, incident reports, and related operational activities through web, iOS and Android applications.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal information when individuals interact with Midbol Care. It also explains our obligations under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Midbol Care is a technology platform. We do not directly provide regulated care services, clinical treatment, medical diagnosis or medication prescribing.

SECTION 06

GPS, Location and Staff Attendance

6.1 When location is collected

Precise GPS location is accessed only when a staff member actively initiates a clock-in or clock-out action. The application checks the staff member's current location against the registered care-service address using geofencing. If the location falls within the permitted geographical area, the clock-in or clock-out action proceeds. If not, the action is restricted.

6.2 What is recorded at clock-in and clock-out

  • Staff identity
  • Date and exact time
  • GPS latitude and longitude (precise)
  • Visit or service-user reference
  • Associated care location

6.3 What is NOT collected

  • Location is not collected while a visit is in progress
  • Location is not monitored while the application runs in the background
  • Location is not collected when the application is closed
  • Managers cannot view staff members' real-time geographical positions
  • Device identifiers and IP addresses are not collected during clock-in/out

6.4 Location retention

GPS location coordinates captured during clock-in and clock-out are used for attendance validation at the point of the action. GPS location history is not separately retained as a standalone record. However, location information is associated with the attendance record created at the time of clock-in or clock-out and is retained as part of that record.

6.5 Location permissions

Users may manage location permissions through their device settings. Disabling location access will prevent the clock-in and clock-out functionality from operating correctly.

SECTION 08

Lawful Bases for Processing

Where BYBL Integrated Services Limited acts as a Data Controller, personal information is processed only where an appropriate lawful basis under Article 6 of the UK GDPR applies. These include:

  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to fulfil contractual obligations
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by applicable law
  • Legitimate interests — for platform security, administration and customer support, where those interests are not overridden by individuals' rights
  • Consent — where consent is the appropriate lawful basis and has been validly obtained

Where health and care information is processed, an additional condition under Article 9 of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 is required. The appropriate condition depends on the care provider's specific processing activities. Care providers are responsible for identifying and documenting the applicable conditions for their own processing.

SECTION 09

Sharing Personal Information

Midbol Care does not use personal information for third-party advertising or cross-application tracking. Personal information may be accessible to:

9.1 Authorised customer personnel

Care workers, managers, administrators and other authorised users, according to their assigned role-based access permissions.

9.2 Midbol Care system administrators

Authorised system administrators may access customer information where necessary for legitimate administrative, security or operational purposes. Developer access to production data is not permitted; synthetic data is used for testing.

9.3 Technology and infrastructure providers

Midbol Care uses Microsoft Azure to host its production environment. A full sub-processor schedule is available on request. Personal information is not transferred to sub-processors for purposes other than those described in this Privacy Policy.

IMPORTANT: The email delivery provider used by Midbol Care must be confirmed and disclosed as a sub-processor. All customer-facing emails should originate from a Midbol Care branded sender address. Please contact us at support@midbolcare.co.uk for the current sub-processor schedule.

9.4 Legal and regulatory authorities

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, a lawful regulatory request or another legally authorised process.

SECTION 10

Hosting and International Data Processing

Midbol Care's production infrastructure is hosted on Microsoft Azure across the following regions:

  • UK South — United Kingdom (primary production environment)
  • West Europe — Netherlands, European Economic Area (EEA) (production services)
  • UK North — United Kingdom (backup storage)

Please note that West Europe is an Azure region located in the Netherlands and is therefore outside the United Kingdom. Accordingly, some personal information may be stored or processed in the EEA as well as the United Kingdom.

The United Kingdom currently recognises the EEA as providing an adequate level of data protection. Transfers to Azure's West Europe region are therefore made on the basis of that adequacy finding. Midbol Care will monitor changes to UK data transfer arrangements and implement any required additional safeguards.

SECTION 11

Data Security

Midbol Care implements the following technical and organisational security measures:

11.1 Encryption

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between users and the platform
  • Database information encrypted at rest
  • Uploaded files and documents encrypted at rest
  • Production backups encrypted at rest

11.2 Access controls

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across all platform modules
  • Multi-Factor Authentication available for all user types
  • Immediate access revocation when a staff member leaves an organisation
  • User session timeout after 15 minutes of inactivity
  • Password hashing using bcrypt

11.3 Audit records

All user actions are recorded in comprehensive, immutable audit logs. Ordinary users cannot alter or delete audit records. Audit logs are retained indefinitely within the platform.

11.4 Environment separation

Development, testing and production environments are separated. Real customer information is not copied into development or testing environments. Synthetic data is used for all testing purposes.

Although appropriate security controls are implemented, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security against all threats.

SECTION 13

Your Rights

Individuals have rights under applicable data protection legislation, subject to relevant legal conditions and exemptions. These include:

  • Right of access — to request a copy of personal information held about you
  • Right to rectification — to request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion in applicable circumstances
  • Right to restrict processing — to request that processing is limited
  • Right to object — to object to certain processing activities
  • Right to data portability — to receive information in a portable format where applicable
  • Right to withdraw consent — where consent is the lawful basis

13.1 Requests concerning care records

Where personal information is controlled by a care organisation, requests should ordinarily be directed to that organisation in the first instance.

13.2 Data export

The platform currently supports information exports in CSV and PDF formats for authorised users. Individual module exports are available within the platform.

13.3 Account and record deletion

Individual users cannot currently delete their own accounts directly through the application. Care organisations do not currently have a self-service function for deleting service-user records. Requests for

deletion or account closure should be directed to support@midbolcare.co.uk and will be assessed in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

SECTION 15

Analytics, Advertising and Automated Decision-Making

Based on the current production configuration of Midbol Care:

  • Midbol Care does not use third-party analytics software on the web platform or mobile applications
  • The mobile applications do not use third-party crash-reporting services
  • The platform does not use session recording, screen replays or heatmap tools
  • Personal information is not used for third-party advertising or cross-application tracking
  • No artificial intelligence functionality is currently active in production
  • Personal care, health or medication information is not sent to any external AI provider
  • The platform does not make automated care, medication, safeguarding or staffing decisions without human review

If any of these practices change in future, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated accordingly.

SECTION 16

Personal Data Breaches

Midbol Care takes data breach identification, containment and reporting seriously.

Where Midbol Care acts as a Data Processor, it will notify the relevant customer organisation without undue delay following awareness of a personal data breach, in accordance with applicable law and the Data Processing Agreement.

Where Midbol Care acts as a Data Controller, it will assess its obligations to notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and affected individuals where legally required. Under UK GDPR, reportable breaches must be notified to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware.

SECTION 17

Backups and Disaster Recovery

Production databases are backed up automatically on a daily basis. Backups are retained for 7 days. Backups are encrypted at rest and stored separately from the production environment in Azure UK North.

Please note that where a record is deleted from the production platform, it may continue to exist in backup copies for up to 7 days before those backups are overwritten.

SECTION 18

Children and Vulnerable Individuals

Midbol Care is intended for professional care-provider organisations and authorised staff. The platform is not designed as a general consumer application for children or members of the public.

However, information concerning children or vulnerable individuals may be processed where a care organisation lawfully uses the platform to manage relevant care services. Care organisations are responsible for establishing appropriate lawful bases, access restrictions and safeguarding procedures for such information.
