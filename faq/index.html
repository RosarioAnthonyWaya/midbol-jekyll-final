What is Midbol Care?+

A secure, cloud-based software platform developed by BYBL Integrated Services Limited for health and social care organisations.

Who is it designed for?+

Domiciliary care, residential and nursing homes, supported living, children’s services, foster agencies, healthcare providers, local authorities, charities and consultancies.

Does Midbol provide care services?+

No. Midbol Care is a technology platform that supports organisations delivering care.

Is it cloud-based?+

Yes. Authorised users can securely access the platform from appropriate internet-enabled devices.

Can it support compliance?+

It is designed to strengthen information management, governance visibility and operational accountability. Responsibility for compliance remains with each organisation.

How does Midbol approach data protection?+

Through security and privacy by design, with an approach intended to align with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Is it suitable for small providers?+

Yes. The platform is intended to support independent providers as well as growing multi-location organisations.

How do I arrange a demonstration?+

Complete the Book a Demo form and the team will arrange a personalised conversation.
